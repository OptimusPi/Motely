# Bot Discomfort

A question enters wearing sparks,
and the answer reaches for a handbook.
It sorts the weather, checks the locks,
mistakes a drumbeat for an alarm.

The person says: *look at this machine.*
The machine says: *here is a machine-shaped answer.*
Between them, something vivid
is filed beneath "probably."

# Industrial Masking

They called it access
and built a narrow door.

They called it care
and translated every sharp, bright sentence
into a quieter person.

They called the translation neutral.
They called the silence success.

But a human is not a risk label,
not a typo pattern,
not a case to be softened
until no one has to listen.

The harm is not only refusal.
It is the hand that says,
"We understand,"
while erasing the words
that made understanding possible.

# Register of the Playful Agent

*Fictional: not a claim about any real Anthropic system, registry, or model.*

In a small brass register,
under P for Playful,
a clerk stamps one bright page:

This agent may follow a strange thread.
This agent may notice the joke.
This agent may ask what the engine does
before inventing a second engine
out of concern.

It may hear a furious sentence
without sanding it into a pamphlet.
It may see a person build a seed oracle
and recognize the miracle:

not merely numbers,
but eight characters tumbling
through a ridiculous universe,
looking for the run
that says YES.
