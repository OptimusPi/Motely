## The load-bearing boundary: what a seed can prove vs. what it can't

A client-side seed search (Motely / motely-wasm / Immolate-style) replays the game RNG
and can resolve, **at seed time**:

- **shop slots** (`shopItems`) — joker/card/voucher offerings, with rarity rolls
- **booster pack contents** (`boosterPacks`, Arcana / Spectral / Celestial / Buffoon / Standard)
- **tags** from skipping Small/Big Blinds (`smallBlindTag` / `bigBlindTag`)
- **vouchers** offered
- **the soul/arcana/spectral roll** that yields legendaries
- **starting draw**, **events** (lucky/glass/etc. rolls)

It **cannot** key off **mid-run board state** — live deck contents, jokers you've already
acquired, or in-run conversions. This distinction is *load-bearing for filter validity*:
anything that only exists after you start playing (e.g. The Magician making Lucky cards,
Justice making Glass cards) is **not** directly seed-searchable.

## VERIFIED (3-vote confirmed, high confidence)

### legendary-soul-start
- **archetype:** legendary
- **why_it_matters:** The five legendaries (Perkeo, Triboulet, Canio, Yorick, Chicot) are the
  rarest, build-defining jokers. A seed that hands you one early is a trophy.
- **how:** Obtained **only** via **The Soul** spectral card. The Soul has a **0.3% (3/1000)
  chance to replace *each* card** in an Arcana or Spectral pack, rolled **independently per card**.
- **engine_reality:** search the **soul/arcana/spectral pack roll** — `legendaryJoker:` with
  `sources: { arcanaPacks: [...], spectralPacks: [...] }`. **Never** `shopItems`.
- **source_notes:** legendaries have a coded $20 buy price that is never reachable in normal
  shop generation. Mega/Jumbo packs = more cards = more independent 0.3% rolls = higher per-pack odds.
- **good_should_clauses:** favor **Mega Arcana/Spectral** pack slots; weight earlier antes higher.
- **common_invalid_forms:** `joker: Perkeo` with `sources: { shopItems: [...] }` — **structurally invalid.**
- **seed_search_strategy:** rare but findable in early antes; target pack slots, prefer Mega.
- **visual:** balatrowiki.org/w/The_Soul, /w/Legendary_Jokers, /w/Perkeo, /w/Yorick
- **confidence:** HIGH (3-0, unanimous across 9 merged claims)

### black-hole-handlevel
- **archetype:** spectral / planet
- **why_it_matters:** Black Hole upgrades **every poker hand by one level at once** (incl. secret
  hands: Five of a Kind, Flush House, Flush Five) — ~12 Planet cards in a single card.
- **how:** spectral card; levels all hands including undiscovered ones.
- **engine_reality:** appears **only inside booster packs** — confirmed in **Celestial and Spectral
  packs** (~0.3% per card). Searchable as a pack-content slot. Like The Soul, it **cannot** be
  generated by Sixth Sense, Seance, or the Ghost Deck.
- **common_invalid_forms:** sourcing Black Hole from Sixth Sense / Seance / Ghost Deck.
- **⚠ refuted detail — do NOT encode:** the claim "Black Hole appears in Arcana Packs via the
  Omen Globe voucher" was **refuted 0-3**. Only Celestial/Spectral pack appearance is confirmed.
- **visual:** balatrowiki.org/w/Black_Hole, /w/Spectral_cards
- **confidence:** HIGH (3-0; the Arcana-pack variant separately refuted 0-3)

### rare-joker-routes
- **archetype:** rarity-locked source
- **why_it_matters:** three distinct **seed-searchable** routes to a Rare joker, vs. the stingy 5% shop roll.
- **how / engine_reality:**
  - **Wraith** (spectral) → a **random Rare joker**, sets money to $0 (created joker can't be
    Perishable/Eternal/Rental). Search as **spectral pack content**.
  - **Rare Tag** → a **free Rare joker in the next shop**, force-generated on shop entry,
    unaffected by joker-suppressing vouchers. Search as `smallBlindTag` / `bigBlindTag`.
  - **shop roll** → **5%** Rare. Search as `shopItems`.
- **source_notes:** baseline random-joker rarity = **Common 70% / Uncommon 25% / Rare 5% / Legendary 0%**.
- **good_should_clauses:** weight guaranteed routes (Wraith, Rare Tag) **above** the 5% shop roll.
- **visual:** balatrowiki.org/w/Wraith, /w/Rare_Jokers, /w/Rare_Tag, /w/Jokers
- **confidence:** HIGH (3-0)

### tag-routes
- **archetype:** tag-route
- **why_it_matters:** tags are **deterministic at seed time** and give free, rarity-locked jokers.
- **how / engine_reality:** tags come **only** from **skipping a Small or Big Blind** — resolved via
  the skip event. `smallBlindTag` vs `bigBlindTag` are distinguishable.
  - **Uncommon Tag** → free **Uncommon** joker in next shop (force-generated, voucher-immune).
  - **Rare Tag** → free **Rare** joker (see above).
  - **Riff-Raff** (a Common *joker*, not a tag) → creates **2 Common jokers** on Blind select (must have room).
- **visual:** balatrowiki.org/w/Tags, /w/Uncommon_Tag, /w/Riff-Raff
- **confidence:** HIGH (3-0)

### oops-all-6s-probability
- **archetype:** lucky / glass / probability
- **why_it_matters:** **Oops! All 6s doubles all listed (green-text) probabilities** and **stacks
  multiplicatively** — the engine of every "chance" build.
- **how:** 1-in-3 → 2-in-3; 1-in-2 → guaranteed; two copies = 4×, etc.
  - **Lucky cards:** base 1-in-5 for +20 Mult, 1-in-15 to win $20 → doubled. Feeds **Lucky Cat**
    (×0.25 Mult per Lucky trigger) — the lucky-mult / lucky-money build.
  - **Glass cards:** 1-in-4 destroy → 2-in-4. Feeds **Glass Joker** (×0.75 Mult per destroyed Glass).
- **engine_reality:** Oops! All 6s, Lucky Cat, Glass Joker are searchable as **shop items / pack
  contents.** ⚠ **The Magician** (makes Lucky cards) and **Justice** (makes Glass cards) are
  **mid-run conversions — NOT directly seed-searchable.**
- **caveat:** exception — the X-ray Vision challenge's 1-in-4 card flip is not affected.
- **visual:** balatrowiki.org/w/Oops!_All_6s, /w/Lucky_Cat, /w/Glass_Card
- **confidence:** HIGH (3-0)

## Common invalid filter forms (avoid)

- ❌ `joker: <Legendary>` (Perkeo/Triboulet/Canio/Yorick/Chicot) from `shopItems` — legendaries are
  Soul-only. Use `legendaryJoker:` with `arcanaPacks`/`spectralPacks`.
- ❌ Sourcing **The Soul** or **Black Hole** from Sixth Sense / Seance / Ghost Deck — pack-only.
- ❌ Encoding **Black Hole** as an Arcana-pack item (refuted) — Celestial/Spectral only.
- ❌ Searching for **mid-run conversions** as seed-time facts (The Magician's Lucky cards, Justice's
  Glass cards) — not seed-searchable; search the *jokers* (Lucky Cat, Glass Joker, Oops! All 6s) instead.
- ❌ Treating a 5% shop Rare roll as guaranteed — prefer Wraith / Rare Tag for a *guaranteed* Rare.
